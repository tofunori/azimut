01Use

Three commands per flight.

The copy of the SD card, the base station's RINEX and, ideally, the CSRS-PPP report for that base. RTKLIB-EX is included.

aCheck

Missing bands, MRK pairing, base coverage. Nothing is changed.

azimut scan flights/ --base base.24o
bCompute

PPK against the base, CGVD2013 heights, quality report.

azimut process flights/ --base base.24o --base-ppp base.sum \ --geoid ca_nrc_CGG2013an83.tif --out results
cUse

Import into PIX4Dmatic, Metashape or ODM, or write into copies of the images.

azimut write-images results/flight_solution.json --mode copy

02Quality report

One flight, one page.

Status, camera map, uncertainty of every capture, base and reference frame. HTML in English or French, and JSON for scripts.

03Features

Between the drone and the orthomosaic.

Azimut replaces the geotagging step. Photogrammetry stays in your software.

Diagnosis
Whole SD cards in seconds, from image headers only.
PPK
RTKLIB-EX; each file at its own exposure time.
Mavic 3M
Bands exposed 26 ms after the RGB image, positioned on their own.
CSRS-PPP & RTK
Base from CSRS-PPP; RTK flights corrected by the shift.
Heights
CGVD2013 or ellipsoidal, geoid checked against the frame.
Outputs
PIX4Dmatic, Metashape, OpenDroneMap, CSV, HTML, JSON.
Images
EXIF and DJI XMP, in place (undoable) or in copies.
Agents
Claude or Codex, with a question at every decision.
